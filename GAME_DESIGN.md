# Пепельный Завет / Ashen Covenant

Original desktop browser 2D action RPG, inspired by heroic fantasy silhouettes and class-based buildcraft. No Blizzard names, characters, music or artwork. Interface language: Russian.

## V1 scope and approved amendment

Five fully playable classes: Warrior, Mage, Hunter, **Necromancer and Assassin added by the owner on 2026-10-16**. Each has a basic attack, four active skills, a passive, two branches containing three talents each. Level cap 15. Four talent points at most: reaching the end of one branch leaves one point for a hybrid, so a single character cannot purchase everything.

Necromancer: limited-duration, capped summons, curse, life drain, persistent plague ground. Branches: Bone Commander / Soul Eater. No corpse-resource bottleneck and no exponential summon growth.
Assassin: melee burst, poison, temporary stealth, directional lunge and area daggers. Branches: Shadow / Poisoner. Stealth ends on offensive actions and has a cooldown; poison refreshes instead of stacking.

Loop: character selection → expedition map / equipment / talents → realtime mission → actual results and rewards → preparation. Five sequentially unlocked missions (no level gates): forest clearance, ruins/Warden boss, cave waves, beacon defense, sanctum/Oracle boss. Replay at normal or veteran difficulty. Enemy level is max(mission level, player level) so earlier missions remain playable without forcing artificial unlock grind. Optional objectives grant +25% mission XP and gold. Defeat retains earned kill XP, pays 20% mission gold, grants no gear or chest; hero revives in camp.

Controls: WASD/arrows, mouse aim and hold LMB, 1–4 skills, Space dodge, E potion, I inventory, C character, K talents, Esc pause. Local single-player; pause on blur/hidden tab; no offline simulation.

Three gear slots, five bounded rarities. Cosmetics are account-wide and never affect stats. Chests from first clears and every third successful expedition, or 160 earned gold. Ten-opening rare-or-better guarantee; duplicates yield shards; 30 shards buy any chosen skin. No real money.

Five differentiated classes; 20 skills; 30 talents; five mission layouts; two phased bosses; seven enemy archetypes plus bosses; 1–15 progression; gear comparison and salvaging; cosmetic collection with actual appearance changes; versioned validated saves and import/export; keyboard-friendly DOM menu; fixed-step combat; automated formula/integration simulations; production build. Runtime visual, performance and human balancing claims require actual evidence.
